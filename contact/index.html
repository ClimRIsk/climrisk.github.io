A limited number of engagements, by design.

ClimRisk takes on a limited number of engagements each quarter to preserve the depth of our diligence. All engagements begin under NDA. Submit the parameters below to start the engagement protocol.

The Engagement Protocol

01

Inquiry

You submit inquiry parameters below. We review scope, sector, and regulatory driver before responding.

02

NDA & Scoping Call

Qualified inquiries receive an NDA and a scoping call to define the asset universe and deliverable.

03

Engagement Proposal

A fixed-scope proposal — timeline, deliverables, and fee — is issued for sign-off before work begins.

04

Delivery

Engine outputs, audit-ready reports, and a working session to defend the methodology to your stakeholders.
