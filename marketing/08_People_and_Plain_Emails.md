## Emails

Rules I followed: short, one ask, one real fact from our own tests, no adjectives. I did not state anything about you I don't know. Add one line about your own background if you want it; it will help.

**1. Leeds Building Society (Graeme McRitchie)**

Subject: A question about how you check climate data

Hi Graeme,

I read that you wanted climate data you could question, not just a score. I'm building a physical climate risk tool that does the opposite of a black box: every hazard shows how well its model held up when tested against what actually happened.

For flooding in England, a ranking from 1 m Environment Agency LiDAR separated flooded from dry buildings with an ROC area of 0.81 to 0.92 on three of the named events we tested, and we publish the ones where it did worse too (York 2020 was 0.63).

I'm not selling anything yet. Would you give me 20 minutes to tell me where this wouldn't hold up for a lender? I'd rather hear it now.

Shri

## Before you send from Zoho

1. Check SPF, DKIM and DMARC are set for climrisk.io in Zoho (Zoho Mail admin, Email Authentication). Cold email from a new domain without them goes to spam.
2. Send no more than 10 to 15 a day for the first two weeks, from the real mailbox, one at a time.
3. Give each person a reply route that is a person: sign with your name and put your LinkedIn in the signature.
4. If you want me to send them: give me a verified address for each, approve the final text, and I'll do it through Zoho in the browser one by one, asking you before each send.
