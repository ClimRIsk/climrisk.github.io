# Pricing tests, partnerships, website and search

## Pricing (hypotheses to test, none of these is evidence)

Buyers will not tell you a price they would pay; they show it by paying or by committing. Test in this order:

1. **Free sample report** on a public site, outreach only.
2. **Paid pilot**, 10 to 25 of their sites over 4 weeks. Try two price points on different accounts, for example a low and a high figure in the low thousands of euros [CONFIRM], and record which converts and what they say.
3. **Annual licence** only after two pilots have repeated. Price by site volume, not users.

Do not publish a price list yet. Quote per pilot.

Cost check: run the engine on 25 sites, time it, and note any paid data cost per report. Open-Meteo's free tier is non-commercial; commercial use may need a paid plan [CONFIRM before the first paid pilot], and every data licence should be checked for commercial use (see `COMPETITIVE_LANDSCAPE.md` and `HAZARD_SOURCE_PROBE.md`).

## Partnerships

| Partner type | Why | First move |
|---|---|---|
| Climate and ESG consultancies | They need a site-level engine behind client work | Offer free reports on a project they are doing; ask for a referral |
| Insurance brokers and MGAs | They explain risk to clients and need independent checks | Cross-check one account against their current tool |
| Valuers and surveyors (property) | Climate risk is entering valuation guidance [CONFIRM current RICS and ESG rules] | Ask for a pilot on a handful of valuations |
| Universities and open-science groups | Independent validation helps credibility | Share the ledger and ask which model they would test |
| Accelerators and angels in climate and fintech | Introductions to the first lenders | Use the YC application and demo video |
