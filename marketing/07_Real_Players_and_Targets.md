## What I did not send

Reports. A test run for Leeds Building Society's HQ rated river flood "negligible". Checked on 27 Oct 2026 against the Environment Agency's recorded flood outlines: that point is 177 m from the nearest outline and outside the December 2015 flood extent, so the rating is not shown to be wrong. (An earlier note here said it was wrong; that was overstated.) The river-depth model is third-party and graded D for small and mid rivers, so the engine also checks LiDAR height above drainage: at Leeds Dock, which did flood in December 2015, it raises river flood to MODERATE. Reports go out only after someone checks the site against the recorded flood outlines.
