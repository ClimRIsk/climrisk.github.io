# Key terms for a short mutual confidentiality agreement (ClimRisk and Charles Hett)

This is a list of points to give a lawyer, not a legal document. Have it reviewed in the jurisdiction that governs ClimRisk B.V.

1. Parties and purpose: ClimRisk B.V. and Charles Hett; purpose limited to reviewing the ClimRisk method and evidence and giving feedback.
2. Confidential information: the evidence pack, any software, model files, data, demonstrations (including screen-share sessions), and the existence of the project; excludes information already public or independently developed.
3. Use: only for the stated purpose; no copying beyond what the review needs; no use to build, train, benchmark or improve a competing product or model.
4. No reverse engineering: no decompiling, extracting, or copying of software, model weights, data or source; no scraping outputs to train another model.
5. Feedback: his feedback may be used by ClimRisk freely; he keeps his own pre-existing knowledge. State whether he is paid and whether any credit is given.
6. Return or deletion: on request or at the end of the review, delete or return everything and confirm in writing.
7. Disclosure: no disclosure to third parties (including employers or clients) without written consent; permitted only where required by law, with notice.
8. Term: confidentiality for a fixed period (for example 3 to 5 years), trade secrets for as long as they remain secret.
9. No licence: nothing transfers any rights; evaluation licences, if any, are for the review only.
10. Remedies and law: injunctive relief; governing law and courts; electronic signature accepted.

Before sending anything: confirm who he is (mutual contacts, professional registration, employer), and keep a dated record of what was sent and when.
