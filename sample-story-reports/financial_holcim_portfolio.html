What this means, by reader

For the lender. Size covenants to the 95th percentile and expected shortfall, not the mean.

For the investor. The mean hides the tail; the tail is where a few coastal assets and the highest-emitting plants sit.

Method and limitations

Each asset run through the four-stage analyst; flood depths fetched live for the top 15 assets by emissions (others: cyclone + policy + transition only). Transition model draws are shared across assets (common scenario uncertainty); physical events independent. % of value is taken per draw against that draw's portfolio value, so it cannot exceed 100%. Direct physical damage and carbon cost only; business interruption is not included. The physical share at company level is approximated as each asset's modelled annual loss times a 25-year annuity at 7%.

How ClimRisk measured this

  • Register → assets, emissions, capacity.
  • Physical → IBTrACS cyclone set, terrain screening, live JRC and Deltares flood depths for the largest emitters.
  • Transition → NGFS price and output paths.
  • Monte Carlo → shared draws, expected loss, percentiles, expected shortfall.
