## About Page Copy

Climate change is a financial risk problem before it is anything else.

Every drought, every flood, every El Niño cycle leaves a financial fingerprint — in commodity markets, in credit portfolios, in enterprise value. Most analysts see the price moves and the loss numbers. Almost none trace them back to the atmospheric physics that caused them.

Physical Finance maps those fingerprints.

Each research brief connects a physical measurement — sea surface temperature anomaly, drought index, flood return period, wildfire intensity — to a specific financial mechanism: EBITDA compression, credit loss probability, infrastructure impairment, enterprise value impact across three NGFS scenarios through 2050.

The data is public. The atmospheric physics is understood. The financial transmission mechanisms are observable in the historical record. What has been missing is the systematic analytical framework that connects the physical to the financial.

That framework is what ClimRisk built. This newsletter is where we publish it.
