**What ClimRisk does**

ClimRisk is a climate financial risk intelligence firm. We built the CRI (Climate Risk Intelligence) engine to answer one specific question: what does physical climate risk actually do to a company's financial performance, expressed in numbers rather than narratives?

The engine works with three inputs: an asset or supply chain's geographic coordinates, physical hazard data from WRI Aqueduct 4.0 and NASA NEX-GDDP CMIP6, and the NGFS Phase 4 transition scenarios. It produces a structured financial output covering EBITDA compression, enterprise value impact, and credit loss probability across three scenarios (Net Zero 2050, Delayed Transition, and Current Policies) through 2050.

The output is a financial model with climate adjustment terms built in. Not a risk score, not an ESG rating, not a qualitative narrative. A DCF with physical climate inputs that analysts can actually use.

**Why this is relevant to Aranca's research offering**

Aranca's clients, including asset managers, private equity funds, and banks, are increasingly required to understand physical climate risk at the asset and company level. TCFD disclosure is standard across most institutional mandates and IFRS S2 is now in force across many jurisdictions. The gap is not that clients lack awareness of climate risk. The gap is that most climate risk tools do not produce financial numbers that analysts can embed into equity notes, credit models, or investment committee materials.

That is the gap ClimRisk closes.
