## Product Description

In 2023 the equatorial Pacific warmed by 2°C above its long-run average.

That single physical measurement — the Niño 3.4 SST anomaly — drove cocoa to a 50-year price high, cut Panama Canal transits by more than half, burned 18 million hectares in Canada, triggered the fourth global mass coral bleaching event, and pushed global insured catastrophe losses past $100 billion for the fourth consecutive year.

Most investors saw the price moves. Almost none traced them back to the atmospheric physics that caused them.

**This report does that.**

It breaks down the 2023 to 2024 Super El Niño from first physical principles — sea surface temperature anomaly, Walker Circulation disruption, ITCZ shift — through to six financial transmission pathways, and then maps those pathways to what the NGFS Phase 4 scenarios say about every El Niño cycle from now to 2050.
